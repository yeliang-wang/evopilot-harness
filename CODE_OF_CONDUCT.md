# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, review technical claims against evidence, accept constructive disagreement, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and free from conflicts of interest. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is not acceptable.
